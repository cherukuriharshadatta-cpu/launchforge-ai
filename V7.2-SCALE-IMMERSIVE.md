# LaunchForge V7.2 — Scale + Immersive Commerce

## What changed

### Bulk inventory pricing
- Search/filter large catalogs.
- Multi-select products and apply common price/stock values.
- Export inventory CSV, edit price/stock in a spreadsheet, import it back.
- Values are written through to Cloudinary structured metadata and remain visible in generated storefronts.

### Media Doctor at scale
- Search box and scrollable list; no hard 8-product limit.

### Creative Lab clarified
- **Campaign composition:** 1–4 product references -> new composed campaign hero via Cloudinary Image Generation.
- **Product scene:** one product -> generative background replacement.
- **Colorway:** one product -> generative recolor preview.
- Generative derived assets are checked for readiness before display to avoid the old blank/black pending preview.

### Product Gallery / 360 / 3D / AR
- Multi-angle SKU groups expose a real 360 spin action.
- Sellers can attach a real GLB/3D model to a SKU.
- Attached models open in Cloudinary Product Gallery with 3D auto-rotate, zoom controls and AR enabled.
- LaunchForge intentionally does **not** claim to reconstruct a true 3D mesh from ordinary photos.

### Metadata + market research
- Visible metadata inspector: Cloudinary IDs, pHash, ETag, focus score, category, price, stock, color, material.
- Market-price buttons open transparent comparable-product searches on Google Shopping, Amazon India and Flipkart.
- Important distinction: Cloudinary metadata stores/searches seller data; it does not contain internet competitor pricing.

### AI Image-to-Video visibility
- Video Studio now has a prominent Image-to-Video beta banner and one-click switch to AI Motion.
